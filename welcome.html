Hi, I'm Mia. I work with couples who would rather be themselves than pose impersonally. I create author documentary films.

I work with respect and support, leaving room for things to unfold naturally.

My eye is cinematic and haute couture. I build the story of your big day through aesthetics and atmosphere. I incorporate sounds, gestures and small details that hold special meaning for you.

If there is a symbol, a metaphor or something that is part of your story, it can also find its place in the film.
